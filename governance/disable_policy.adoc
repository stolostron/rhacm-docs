[#disable-policy]
= Disabling security policies

[role="_abstract"]
Your policy is enabled by default. Disable a policy from the console without deleting it.

.Procedure

. Log in to your console, navigate to the _Governance_ page to view a table list of your policies.

. Select the *Actions* icon > *Disable policy*. The _Disable Policy_ dialog box appears.

. Click *Disable policy*. Your policy is disabled.
