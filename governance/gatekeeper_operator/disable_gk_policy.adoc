[#disabling-gk-operator-policy]
= Disabling {gate} policy

[role="_abstract"]
Disable your `policy-gatekeeper-operator` policy from the console or command line interface.

*Required access*: Cluster administrator

.Procedure

. To disable your policy from the console, select the *Disable* option from the _Actions_ menu for the `policy-gatekeeper-operator` policy.

. To disable your policy from the command line interface, set `spec.disabled: true` on the policy resource.
