[#etcd-encryption-policy]
= etcd encryption policy

[role="_abstract"]
The `etcd-encryption` policy detects or enables encryption of sensitive data in the etcd data store. The Kubernetes configuration policy controller monitors the status of the `etcd-encryption` policy.

*Note:* The etcd encryption policy only supports {ocp-short} 4 and later.
