= E8 scan policy

[role="_abstract"]
The Essential 8 (E8) scan policy references the E8 security profiles used to evaluate master and worker nodes. You must install the Compliance Operator to apply the E8 scan policy.

The E8 scan policy is a Kubernetes configuration policy in {acm-short}. {ocp-short} supports the E8 scan policy.
