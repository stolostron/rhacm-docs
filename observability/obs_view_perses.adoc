[#view-perses-dashboards]
= Viewing Perses dashboards in the console

[role="_abstract"]
View hub cluster and managed cluster metrics in Perses dashboards from the *Observe* menu in the {ocp-short} console on your {acm-short} hub cluster.

*Required access:* Cluster administrator

.Prerequisites

* The observability service is enabled on your hub cluster. See xref:../observability/observability_enable.adoc#enabling-observability-service[Enabling the observability service].

* The Perses dashboards are enabled in the `MultiClusterObservability` custom resource. See xref:../observability/obs_enable_mcoa_perses.adoc#enable-mcoa-perses[Enabling Perses dashboards with the multicluster observability add-on (Technology Preview)].

.Procedure

. Log in to the {ocp-short} console on your hub cluster.

. From the navigation menu, click *Observe*.

. Click *Dashboards (Perses)*.

. On the dashboard list page, select the `open-cluster-management-observability` project from the project drop-down list.

. Click a dashboard name to view it. For example, select *ACM Clusters Overview* to view a fleet-wide summary of your managed clusters, including cluster count, resource utilization, and status.

. Use the variable drop-down lists at the top of each dashboard to filter the data by cluster, namespace, or other dimensions, depending on the dashboard.

*Note:* In addition to the {acm-short} dashboards that are deployed automatically, you can create custom Perses dashboards to visualize additional metrics collected by the multicluster observability add-on. Use `ScrapeConfig` resources to collect custom metrics from your managed clusters, and then build Perses dashboards to visualize them.
