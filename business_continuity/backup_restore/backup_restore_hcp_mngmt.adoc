[#restore-hcp-management]
= Restoring {hcp} management configuration

[role="_abstract"]
When restoring hosted clusters that are managed by {acm-short}, follow steps to ensure a successful recovery of both the {acm-short} hub cluster and the hosted clusters.

.Procedure

. Restore the {acm-short} hub cluster. See _Restoring a backup_ from the _Additional resources_ section.
. Deploy new {acm-short} hub with same configuration as the original. See _Installing the backup and restore operator_ from the _Additional resources_ section.
. Enable backup component and configure OADP.
. Restore {acm-short} backup by using the `cluster-backup-operator`. 
. Verify discovery and auto-import policies are restored. See _Discovering {mce-short} hosted clusters in {acm-short}_ from the _Additional resources_ section.

. Verify that {mce-short} hosted environment is reconnected to the restored {acm-short} hub cluster. See _Restoring a backup_ from the _Additional resources_ section. The following clusters and resources are restored when the backup and restore is successful:
    - {mce-short} hosting clusters automatically reconnect to restored {acm-short} hub cluster.
    - The `hypershift-addon` resumes discovery operations.
    - Previously discovered hosted clusters are rediscovered.

. Recover your hosted cluster. Hosted clusters require a separate recovery by using individual backup procedures. The {acm-short} hub cluster automatically rediscovers clusters after the APIs become available. See _OpenShift Hosted Control Plane Disaster Recovery documentation_ from the _Additional resources_ section.
