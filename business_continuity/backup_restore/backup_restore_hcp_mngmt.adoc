[#restore-hcp-management]
= Restoring {hcp} management configuration

[role="_abstract"]
When restoring hosted clusters that are managed by {acm-short}, follow steps to ensure a successful recovery of both the {acm-short} hub cluster and the hosted clusters.

Follow link:https://docs.redhat.com/en/documentation/openshift_container_platform/4.20/html/hosted_control_planes/high-availability-for-hosted-control-planes[OpenShift Hosted Control Plane Disaster Recovery documentation].

.Procedure

See the following guidance to restore {hcp} management configuration

. Restore the {acm-short} hub cluster. See xref:../backup_restore/backup_restore.adoc[Restoring a backup].
. Deploy new {acm-short} hub with same configuration as the original. See xref:../backup_restore/backup_install.adoc[Installing the backup and restore operator]
. Enable backup component and configure OADP.
. Restore {acm-short} backup by using the `cluster-backup-operator`. 
. Verify discovery and auto-import policies are restored. See link:../../mce_acm_integration/acm_hosted_cluster/acm_discover_hosted.adoc[Discovering {mce-short} hosted clusters in {acm-short}]

. Verify that {mce-short} hosted environment is reconnected to the restored {acm-short} hub cluster. See xref:../backup_restore/backup_restore.adoc[Restoring a backup]. The following clusters and resources are restored when the backup and restore is successful:
    - {mce-short} hosting clusters automatically reconnect to restored {acm-short} hub cluster.
    - The `hypershift-addon` resumes discovery operations.
    - Previously discovered hosted clusters are rediscovered.

. Recover your hosted cluster. Hosted clusters require a separate recovery by using individual backup procedures. The {acm-short} hub cluster automatically rediscovers clusters after the APIs become available. See link:https://docs.redhat.com/en/documentation/openshift_container_platform/4.20/html/hosted_control_planes/high-availability-for-hosted-control-planes[OpenShift Hosted Control Plane Disaster Recovery documentation].

For other guidance on backup and restore, see the xref:../backup_restore/backup_intro.adoc[Backup and restore] documentation.

For more information about {hcp}, see link:../../clusters/about/mce_hcp_overview.adoc[About hosted control planes with {mce-short}].
