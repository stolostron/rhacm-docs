= Updating to {gitops} 1.17 causes permission errors

[role="_abstract"]
When you update your {gitops-short} operator to version 1.17, you get permission errors in your `ApplicationSet` resource status. To workaround this issue, complete the following steps:

.Procedure

. Go to the `openshift-gitops` namespace and delete the `RoleBinding` resource named `openshift-gitops-applicationset-controller-placement`. 
. Restart the `multicluster-operators-application-\*\` pod in the `open-cluster-management` namespace.

.Additional resources
